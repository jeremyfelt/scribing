    How to scribe

    A page of your own words

    1. Scribe words

      Swipe through touching letters, in any direction, diagonals too. Three letters or more. Tapping letters one by one works as well; tap the last letter again to scribe.

    2. Fill the page

      Each word takes a line. Reach the page’s target before your lines run out and the page is illuminated. Long words score far more than short ones.

    3. Keep a running hand

      Begin a word with the last letter of the word before it to join them. Each joined word multiplies: ×1.5, ×2, ×2.5 and on. Tiles that would join are marked with a red corner.

    4. Beasts in the margin

      After each illuminated page, adopt one of three marginal beasts. They bend the rules. You can keep five.
